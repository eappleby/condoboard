What goes in the email

The summary always has three parts. Anything overdue, in full. The month ahead, in full, with who is responsible, vendor contacts, documents and costs. Then the month after that, as a short list of names and who has them.

Sent on or before the 20th, the detailed month is the current one. From the 21st it is the next month, which suits a board sending near month end. The email always names the months it covers.
